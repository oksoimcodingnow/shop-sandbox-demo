Concept prototype

Shop Sandbox

Small restaurants see their sales, not what is left after rent, wages, waste and their own unpaid time. Shop Sandbox shows an owner their whole business, then lets them rehearse a decision or a shock before it costs real money.

  1. Notification: the coach speaks up when a decision is due, such as a price war nearby or a cost rise.
  2. Today: one number, what the shop really keeps, and the coach's cards: a price war, a cheaper supplier, milk up 20%, the 60/40 scheme ending, and more ideas.
  3. Compare: two to four options, each with one number, and what customers nearby pay per order. The coach picks the best result even if customers react badly.
  4. Try small: a capped test on the platform, or a saved plan for a shock.
  5. Result: what happened, the lesson, the skill it practised and a learning streak.

Every rehearsal practises one of four skills: know your true profit, read the market, test before you spend, plan for a shock. The coach suggests; the owner decides.

One tap away: My month, where the money goes (fixed, operating and hidden costs, from platform data, receipt photos and a few numbers by voice), and Ask the coach, which stops off-topic questions at a topic gate.

How the numbers work

  • The shop is made up. A month is 30 days with 26 open days.
  • Commission is 30% plus 7% VAT on it, charged on the price after the shop's own discount. The basis is our assumption.
  • Staff earn ฿60 an hour plus 5% employer social security.
  • Electricity is estimated from the national average rate of ฿3.95 a unit before VAT (Sep to Dec 2026).
  • Hidden costs: waste at 4% of ingredients, refunds at 1% of platform sales, staff drinks, and a ฿250,000 fit-out wearing out over 5 years. All are estimates.
  • The owner's fair wage is set at ฿12,000 a month. That is our assumption.
  • Demand responds to price with an elasticity of 0.8, 1.6 or 2.4 on the platform, and 60% of that in the shop. The lifts from longer hours, help, the blender and the new drink are our assumptions.
  • Loan terms use 2.75% a month, the maximum Grab Thailand published for merchant cash loans in June 2025.
  • Wongnai's own example coffee shop makes ฿10,000 a month on ฿180,000 of sales, before counting its ฿1.1m set-up cost (Wongnai for Business, 29 Aug 2025).
  • Test results, receipt reading and voice input are simulated. The coach chat is scripted; its gate scores are illustrative.
  • The order-value bands, the repeat-customer rates and the busiest hours are made up.
  • Suppliers, their prices and ratings, and the ฿40 delivery fee are made up. Fresh milk is held back until a cold chain exists.
  • Off-topic messages are stopped by a topic gate, a small model that compares each message with shop questions, before they reach the main model.
