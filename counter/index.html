Shop Counter

A delivery platform sees only what is sold through it. In this sample shop that is about a fifth of the sales. Shop Counter is a free till on the owner's phone: it counts every sale at the counter, so the shop's whole month fills in by itself.

  1. Sell: tap the drinks, take cash or a QR payment. The sale is counted.
  2. Today: cups by hour, the busiest hours, and how much of the shop the platform can see.
  3. Coach: what the count says about growing, with a button to rehearse it in Shop Sandbox before spending or borrowing.
  4. Stages: Start and Grow are free and unlocked by use. Scale is paid, for shops big enough to need staff, stock and branches.

The coach suggests; the owner decides. A loan is suggested only as something to rehearse first.

How the numbers work

  • It is the same made-up shop as in Shop Sandbox. Prices and cups a day match it.
  • A month is 26 open days. Platform sales are the weekly platform orders from Shop Sandbox.
  • Cups by hour, the last 7 days, the queue and the walk-away figures are made up.
  • The second blender costs ฿14,000. Loan terms use 2.75% a month, the maximum Grab Thailand published for merchant cash loans in June 2025.
  • The stages, their conditions and the ฿299 price are our proposal, not a product or a price of any platform.
  • No payment is taken: "QR payment" only records the sale.

Made for a GrabSpark 2026 case-competition entry. Not affiliated with or made by Grab or any delivery platform. Every shop, price and neighbourhood figure is sample data.
